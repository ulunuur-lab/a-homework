Present Perfect vs Past Simple (Farqlash Trenajyori)

Tugagan o'tgan vaqt (Past Simple) bilan natijasi hozirga bog'liq bo'lgan yoki vaqti aytilmagan tajriba (Present Perfect: have/has + V3) o'rtasidagi farq hamda 31–60 noto'g'ri fe'llar interaktiv trenajyori.

⏰ 1. FINISHED vs UNFINISHED TIME
Vaqt kalit so'zlariga qarab zamonni tanlang:
• Past Simple: yesterday, last night/year, in 2021, 2 hours ago.
• Present Perfect: today, this week, ever, never, already, yet, just, so far.
🌍 2. LIFE EXPERIENCE (Hayotiy tajriba)
Qachon bo'lgani noma'lum tajriba vs Aniq vaqt:
• "Have you ever been to Paris?" (hayotingizda borganmisiz?)
• "Yes, I went there in 2022." (aniq vaqt aytilsa — Past Simple!)
⏳ 3. FOR / SINCE vs AGO
Hozir ham davom etyaptimi yoki tugaganmi?
• "I have lived here for 5 years." (hali ham shu yerda yashayman)
• "I moved here 5 years ago." (5 yil oldin ko'chib kelganman — tugagan)
🚪 4. GONE TO vs BEEN TO
Hozir shu yerdami yoki yo'q?
• "He has gone to Tashkent." (u ketgan, hozir shu yerda yo'q)
• "He has been to Tashkent." (borib kelgan, tajribasi bor)
⚠️ O'ZBEKCHA QOPQON (Eng xavfli xato):
O'zbek tilida "Men o'tgan yili borganman" degan gap to'g'ri eshitiladi, lekin ingliz tilida: "I have been to London last year" ❌ — KATTA XATO! Agar gapda last year, yesterday, in 2020, ago kabi aniq o'tgan vaqt bo'lsa, qat'iy ravishda Past Simple (I went to London last year ✅) ishlatilishi shart!
