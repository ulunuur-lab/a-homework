Present Perfect Continuous & 30 ta Noto'g'ri Fe'l (A1)

Present Perfect Continuous zamonini mukammal o'rganing: o'tmishda boshlanib, hozir ham davom etayotgan harakatlar! Shuningdek, ingliz tilining eng muhim ikkinchi guruhidagi 30 ta noto'g'ri fe'llarni amaliy mashqlar orqali mustahkamlang.

1️⃣ PPC FORMULASI
I / You / We / They → have been + V-ing
He / She / It / Aziz → has been + V-ing
"I have been waiting" / "She has been cooking"
2️⃣ FOR vs SINCE
FOR = Davomiylik (for 2 hours, for 5 days)
SINCE = Boshlangan vaqt (since morning, since 2021)
"for three days" vs "since yesterday"
3️⃣ 30 IRREGULAR VERBS (LATTER)
Noto'g'ri fe'llarning V2 va V3 shakllarini eslab qoling
make → made → made | see → saw → seen
take → took → taken | write → wrote → written
